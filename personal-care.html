Dignity, choice and comfort

Support with the things that make a day yours.

Our carers offer patient, one-to-one support with everyday activities while encouraging independence. We’ll discuss what you would like help with, how you prefer things to be done and who you want involved.

  • Bathing, washing and personal hygiene
  • Dressing, grooming and getting ready for the day
  • Toileting and discreet continence support
  • Mobility and safe movement around the home
  • Wellbeing check-ins and agreed daily routines

The exact support available is agreed during assessment and depends on individual needs and the care plan.
