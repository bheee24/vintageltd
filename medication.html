Support that fits your care plan.

Medication needs are different for everyone. We’ll talk through the routine with you and the people you choose, and agree what support is appropriate and how it will be recorded.

  • Agreed prompts and reminders as part of a care visit
  • Practical routine support set out in the care plan
  • Clear recording and communication in line with policy
  • Escalation of concerns to the appropriate person
  • Coordination with family and professionals where agreed

Safety comes first

Medication support is only provided following assessment and in line with the agreed care plan, staff training and applicable procedures. We do not replace advice from a doctor or pharmacist.
